## FAQ: How do I create an account?
Creating an account is easy! Just download the app from the App Store or Google Play, and follow the on-screen instructions. You will be asked to provide your name, email address, and phone number.

## FAQ: What analytics features are available on TEEP?
TEEP provides real-time insights into the sum of your usage, your expenses by category, and financial reports to help you make data-driven decisions.

## FAQ: How can I use TEEP to pay my bills?
To pay your bills with TEEP, simply log in to your account, navigate to the "dashboard" section, select the bill category, and follow the prompts to complete the payment using your preferred payment method.

## FAQ: How do I top up my airtime or data using TEEP?
Topping up your airtime or data with TEEP is easy. Navigate to the "Top Up" section, select your mobile network, enter the desired amount, and proceed with the payment to instantly recharge your airtime or data.
